01 — The Operational Bottleneck

Paper ledgers, forgotten kitchen chits, and midnight cash arguments.

One Shot Snooker & Gaming Arena operates 8 full-size tournament snooker and pool tables alongside an in-house cafe. During peak evening hours, up to 35 customers compete simultaneously.

Before FIXIA, the club relied on manual paper logs. Waitstaff noted start times on notepads, kitchen orders were scribbled on paper chits, and table rates were calculated with handheld desk calculators.

The result was financial chaos: customers contested table minutes when billed, cafe orders were served without being logged onto the final bill, and end-of-shift cash balancing took 2.5 hours of disputes every night.

02 — The Architectural Solution

A real-time session matrix with sub-10ms synchronization.

FIXIA designed and built CueDesk, a custom operational platform tailored strictly to venue workflow. Every table is represented as an interactive card displaying live elapsed minutes, current tariff, active players, and linked kitchen tickets.

Core Engineered Modules
  • 8-Table Session Matrix: One-tap start, pause, transfer, and end with automated per-minute rate rounding.
  • Integrated POS Kitchen Ticketing: Beverages and snacks automatically attach to the active table session.
  • Staff Expense & Cash Drawer: Live ledger tracking petty cash in/out and net cash in drawer.
  • Automated Shift Close: 1-click end-of-day reconciliation generating a complete P&L summary.

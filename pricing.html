Clear Answers Upfront

Questions people ask before starting.

Do I really own 100% of the code and intellectual property?

Yes, completely. Unlike subscription SaaS where you rent your software and lose everything if you cancel, FIXIA hands over full source code, database schemas, and intellectual property rights to your company upon milestone completion. You can host it anywhere, modify it, or bring developers in-house at any time.

What if I don't know the exact technical requirements yet?

That is our job. You do not need technical specifications, wireframes, or architecture documents. Tell us how your business works, what slows you down, or what product you want to launch. Within 48 hours, we translate your bottleneck into a visual blueprint, user journey, and fixed-milestone scope.

How does fixed-milestone billing work?

We divide every project into 2–3 transparent milestones (e.g. Discovery & Interactive Prototype, Core Features & Integration, Final Polish & Launch). You only pay each milestone upon reviewing and approving the working progress on your staging link.

Can you integrate with tools we already use?

Yes. We regularly connect custom systems to WhatsApp Cloud API, Razorpay, UPI QR, Google Sheets, and custom third-party APIs so your team doesn't have to change their existing operations.

What happens after the product launches?

We train your team, monitor real user sessions, and provide 30 days of complimentary bug-fix warranty. If you want ongoing technology partnership, we offer monthly retainers covering managed cloud hosting, 24/7 uptime monitoring, and weekly feature sprints.
